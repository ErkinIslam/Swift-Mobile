FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Will a mobile oil change affect my car's warranty?

No. Getting your oil changed by us doesn't void your car's warranty, as long as the right oil is used, and we always use the type and viscosity recommended for your car. Every service comes with an invoice, and that invoice is your proof of maintenance for your warranty.

What oil do you use?

When we bring the oil, we buy it from a parts store like AutoZone and make sure it's the type and viscosity recommended for your make and model. If your car's manufacturer recommends a specific brand, we buy that brand. You pay the same price we pay at the store, with no upcharge. Want a particular brand or type? Just write it in the notes when you book and we'll get the oil you want. Or provide your own oil and filter and pay just $99 + tax.

Can you use an engine flush or oil additives?

Yes, if you request it. You can provide the product yourself, or we can buy it for you: just mention it in the notes when you book, including a specific product if you have one in mind. There's no extra labor charge, and you pay the store price with no upcharge.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes. Every service comes with an invoice that summarizes the work done, the type and brand of oil used and its cost, and the labor cost. If we notice anything wrong with your vehicle, we'll mention it to you and note it on the invoice too.

What do you need from me?

Just some space to park our service vehicle, access to where your car is parked, and your car keys. That's it.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Just call or text us and we'll cancel or find a new time.

What if it rains, or you have to cancel?

We usually work through light rain. We cover your vehicle so no water gets into the engine bay, and only cancel for really heavy rain. If we need to cancel or reschedule because of the weather or anything else that gets in the way, we'll let you know as soon as possible and set up a new time.

How do I pay?

You pay after the job is done. We accept cash, card, Zelle, Venmo and Cash App.
